Debout wakes the computers on your home network with Wake-on-LAN, and tells you honestly whether it worked.

<b>One gesture</b>
Each machine fills the screen. Hold anywhere for half a second: its eyes open, the magic packets leave, and Debout watches the machine start. Swipe to go from one machine to the next.

<b>Always honest</b>
Debout checks whether each machine is on, asleep or not answering, and follows a wake until the machine replies, with an optional notification.

<b>Quick access everywhere</b>
Your first three machines get Quick Settings tiles and app shortcuts: a tap wakes them.

<b>Pair in seconds</b>
On your computer, open debout.sybers.fr and run one command (Windows, macOS or Linux). It finds the network adapter, checks the Wake-on-LAN settings, offers to fix them, and shows a QR code to scan. You can also enter a machine by hand.

<b>Help when it does not wake</b>
A short checklist for each system: BIOS, network adapter, fast startup, Ethernet rather than Wi-Fi.

<b>Free and respectful</b>
No ads, no account, no tracking, no location permission. Open source under GPL-3.0.
