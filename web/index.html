Debout

Wake your computers from your Android phone.

1. Get the app

Download the APK

Free and open source. Install it directly, or keep it up to date with Obtainium.

2. Pair your computer

On the PC to wake, open PowerShell and run:

irm debout.sybers.fr/win | iex

The script finds your network adapter, checks Wake-on-LAN and offers to fix what it can, with your confirmation. It then opens a page with a QR code: in Debout, tap “Scan a QR code”.

Read the script

On the Mac to wake, open Terminal and run:

curl -fsSL debout.sybers.fr/mac | sh

The script finds your network interface, checks “Wake for network access” and offers to turn it on, with your confirmation. It then opens a page with a QR code: in Debout, tap “Scan a QR code”.

A Mac wakes from sleep, not from a full shutdown, and waking is not always reliable on a Mac.

Read the script

On the machine to wake, run:

curl -fsSL debout.sybers.fr/linux | sh

The script finds your network interface, checks Wake-on-LAN with ethtool and offers to turn it on, with your confirmation. It then shows the QR code to scan in Debout: in the terminal if qrencode is installed, otherwise on a web page.
