Debout

Wake your computers from your Android phone.

1. Get the app

Download the APK

Free and open source. Install it directly, or keep it up to date with Obtainium.

2. Pair your computer

On the PC to wake, open PowerShell and run:

irm debout.sybers.fr/win | iex

The script finds your network adapter, checks Wake-on-LAN and offers to fix what it can, with your confirmation. It then opens a page with a QR code: in Debout, tap “Scan a QR code”.
